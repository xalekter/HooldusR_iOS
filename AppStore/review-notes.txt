APP REVIEW NOTES — HooldusR
Paste into App Store Connect > your version > App Review Information > Notes.
No sign-in or demo account is required.

WHAT THE APP IS
HooldusR is a forest navigation tool made by Tartu Observatory (University of
Tartu). It displays a map layer derived from remote sensing data that highlights
forest stands which should be inspected on the ground for maintenance cuttings.
A forester navigates to a highlighted stand by GPS, checks it in person, and
records whether the analysis was right.

IMPORTANT — THE MAP LAYER COVERS ESTONIA ONLY
The coloured overlay is served from a map server holding remote sensing analysis
for the territory of Estonia. Outside Estonia the app correctly shows Apple's
base map with no overlay on top. This is expected behaviour, not a bug or an
unfinished feature.

To see the overlay, please pan the map to Estonia, for example:
    latitude 58.30, longitude 26.51   (near Tartu, Estonia)
Zoom out one or two steps after arriving and the coloured areas become obvious.
A screenshot of the expected result is attached to this submission.

WHAT THE COLOURS MEAN
Red    — analysis produced up to 2023
Orange — area updated in 2024

HOW TO EXERCISE EVERY FEATURE
1. Allow location when prompted. Current coordinates appear in the black strip
   at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap the location arrow (far left) to centre the map on your position.
3. Tap the tree icon to hide and show the analysis layer.
4. Tap the globe/map icon to switch between standard and satellite base maps.
5. Tap "+" to record an observation. Choose Correct, Incorrect or Missing, add
   an optional comment, tap Save. The observation is stored locally at your
   current coordinates.
6. Open the "..." menu and enable "Show points" to draw saved observations as
   coloured markers. Tap a marker, then the blue detail button in its callout,
   to delete it.
7. Open the "..." menu and choose "Save as *.csv" to export every observation
   through the standard iOS share sheet.

WHY LOCATION IS REQUIRED
Location is the core of the app. Each observation is a geographic point recorded
where the forester is standing, and the map follows their position as they walk
to a highlighted stand. The Save button stays disabled without a GPS fix.
Location is used only while the app is in use; there is no background use.

PRIVACY
All observations are stored in a local SQLite file in the app's own Documents
directory. Nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or
third-party SDKs. The only outbound requests fetch map tiles from
https://nutriloopworks.to.ee and carry no personal data. The CSV export leaves
the device only if the user chooses to share it.
