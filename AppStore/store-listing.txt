DESCRIPTION [4000]
HooldusR is a forest navigation app for finding and verifying stands that may
need maintenance cuttings. It is developed at Tartu Observatory, University of
Tartu.

The app draws a map layer derived from remote sensing data covering the whole
territory of Estonia. The layer highlights places that should be checked on the
ground for maintenance cutting. You navigate to a highlighted stand by GPS,
inspect it in situ, and record what you found without leaving the map.

MAP COLOURS
Highlighted areas are coloured by the year the analysis was produced:
• Red — produced up to 2023
• Orange — area updated in 2024

WHAT YOU RECORD
At your exact position you save one of three values:
• Correct — the stand does need a maintenance cutting, as the analysis indicated
• Incorrect — the analysis flagged this stand, but no cutting is needed
• Missing — a cutting is needed here, but the analysis did not flag it

Each observation stores the date, your coordinates and an optional comment.

IN THE FIELD
• Live GPS position with coordinates shown to six decimal places
• Standard or satellite base map, switched with one tap
• Hide the analysis layer to read the base map underneath
• Saved observations drawn on the map as green, red and yellow markers
• Delete an observation recorded by mistake
• Export the whole survey as a CSV file and send it from your phone

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Observations are stored in a database on your device. Nothing is uploaded, there
are no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Your survey leaves the phone
only when you export it and choose where to send it.

COVERAGE
The remote sensing layer covers the territory of Estonia. Elsewhere the app works
as a plain map and will still record observations, but no analysis layer is drawn.

HooldusR is the iOS companion to the Android app of the same name.

APP PRIVACY QUESTIONNAIRE
"Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?" → NO,
choose "Data Is Not Collected".

Reasoning if queried: the app reads device location and writes it to a local
file. Apple defines collection as transmitting data off the device. HooldusR
transmits nothing; the CSV export is a user-initiated share, which Apple
excludes from collection.
