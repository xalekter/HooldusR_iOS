## Who we are

HooldusR is developed by Tartu Observatory, University of Tartu, Estonia.

Contact: [EMAIL]

## The short version

HooldusR does not collect your data. Everything the app records stays on your
phone until you choose to export it and send it somewhere yourself.

## Location

The app uses your device's location while you are using it, for one purpose: to
show your position on the forest map and to record the coordinates of the
observation points you save.

Your location is written to a database file inside the app's own storage on your
device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

The app does not use location in the background.

## What is stored on your device

Each observation point you save contains a timestamp, a detection value
(Correct, Incorrect or Missing), latitude and longitude, and any comment you
type. These are stored in a local database file. Deleting the app deletes them.

## Exporting your data

You can export all your observation points as a CSV file. The file is created on
your device and handed to the standard iOS share sheet, where you decide what
happens to it. Whatever you send it to — mail, a cloud drive, another app — is
governed by that service's own privacy policy, not ours.

## Network requests

The app downloads map images from a map server operated by the project at
`nutriloopworks.to.ee`. These requests ask for map tiles covering a geographic
area. They do not contain your identity, your account details, or your saved
observations. The server's access logs may record the requesting IP address, as
is standard for any web server.

The app also loads base map data from Apple Maps, covered by
[Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## What we do not do

- No user accounts
- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
- No third-party SDKs
- No sale or sharing of personal data

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.

## Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you a copy of
or to delete. To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app from your
device.
